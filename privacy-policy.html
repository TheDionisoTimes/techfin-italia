Privacy Policy — DionisoHermes App

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account to DionisoHermes, we collect:

  • Basic profile information: Your TikTok username and avatar (via Login Kit).
  • OAuth tokens: Access and refresh tokens to publish content on your behalf.

We do not collect or store: email address, phone number, direct messages, follower lists, or payment information.

2. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate your identity and verify the connected account.
  • To upload and publish AI-generated short videos to your TikTok account.
  • To display your connected account name in our internal dashboard.

3. Data Storage and Security

OAuth tokens are stored securely and encrypted. All data is processed on our private server. We do not share your data with any third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses TikTok's official APIs (Login Kit, Content Posting API, Video Publishing Kit) and Composio for OAuth management. These services have their own privacy policies.

5. Content Publishing

The App publishes a maximum of 1 video per day to your TikTok account. Videos are AI-generated and contain original content (narrated stories with gameplay backgrounds). You can remove any published video at any time through TikTok.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: You can request information about the data we hold about you.
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your data at any time.
  • Revocation: You can revoke the App's access at any time in your TikTok settings.

7. Data Retention

We retain your OAuth tokens only as long as the App is connected to your account. Upon revocation, tokens are deleted immediately.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for users under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
